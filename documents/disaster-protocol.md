# Barangay Tinago Disaster Preparedness Protocol

## Introduction

The Barangay Tinago Disaster Preparedness Protocol is devised to enhance the community's resilience and capacity to respond effectively to emergencies and disasters. This document establishes a structured approach for disaster risk management, defines preparedness measures, and outlines key roles and responsibilities. Our objective is to safeguard the lives, properties, and livelihoods of the residents through proactive planning and community engagement.

## Part I: Disaster Risk Identification and Preparedness

### Section 1: Community Risk Assessment

#### 1.1 Hazard Mapping and Risk Zones

- Create detailed maps identifying flood-prone areas, landslide risks, and earthquake fault lines within Barangay Tinago.
- Mark out critical infrastructure and social facilities that are susceptible to various hazards.

#### 1.2 Historical Data Review

- Compile records of past natural and man-made disasters affecting the barangay, noting patterns and lessons learned.
- Use this data to predict and prepare for future emergencies.

#### 1.3 Potential Impact Analysis

- Assess the potential impacts of identified hazards on the barangay's population, infrastructure, and economy.
- Develop scenarios to guide the community's preparation and response.

### Section 2: Disaster Preparedness Plans

#### 2.1 Community-Based Disaster Risk Management Strategy

- Formulate plans that address the unique needs and capacities of Barangay Tinago.
- Engage community members in the development and implementation of these strategies.

#### 2.2 Early Warning Systems and Communication

- Implement a barangay-level early warning system utilizing available technologies and local networks.
- Establish communication protocols to disseminate warnings effectively to all residents.

#### 2.3 Evacuation Plans and Routes

- Design evacuation routes and procedures that cater to the safe and rapid movement of people.
- Clearly mark these routes and regularly disseminate information to the community.

#### 2.4 Resource Inventory

- Maintain an up-to-date record of available emergency resources including shelters, relief goods, and medical supplies.
- Ensure these resources are accessible and sufficient for the barangay's population.

### Section 3: Capacity Building and Training

#### 3.1 Education and Training for Residents

- Conduct regular educational seminars on disaster risk reduction and management.
- Distribute informative materials that explain preparedness measures and response actions.

#### 3.2 Skills Development for Response Teams

- Offer training workshops for emergency response teams that cover search and rescue, first aid, and disaster management.
- Certify participants and regularly refresh their skills through follow-up training.

#### 3.3 Drills and Simulation Exercises

- Coordinate with local disaster management authorities to conduct drills that simulate various disaster scenarios.
- Use these exercises to assess the community's preparedness and refine response protocols.

### Section 4: Barangay Emergency Response Team (BERT)

#### 4.1 Team Structure and Roles

- Define the organizational structure and individual roles within BERT.
- Clarify the chain of command and designate responsibilities during disaster events.

#### 4.2 Coordination with Local Government Units

- Formalize arrangements for cooperation with LGUs and national agencies for disaster response and resource allocation.
- Plan joint trainings and coordination meetings to ensure cohesive action during emergencies.

#### 4.3 Volunteer Recruitment and Management

- Develop a program for recruiting and managing volunteers that includes registration, training, and mobilization plans.
- Establish a database of volunteers categorized by skills and availability.

### Section 5: Conclusion

Summarize the importance of the disaster preparedness protocols, and encourage ongoing community participation and feedback. Reaffirm the commitment to continually update the protocols based on evolving risks and community needs.

## Part II: Emergency Response and Recovery Protocols

### Section 1: Activation and Mobilization

#### 1.1 Protocol for Activating Barangay Disaster Response Team (BERT)

- Define clear trigger points for the activation of BERT based on hazard intensity and early warning signals.
- Communicate the activation to team members, local government units, and residents using predefined channels.

#### 1.2 Mobilization of Community Resources and Volunteers

- Establish procedures for the immediate mobilization of community resources and volunteers.
- Assign roles and dispatch resources according to the action plans set in place for different disaster scenarios.

### Section 2: Incident Command System

#### 2.1 Establishment of Barangay Disaster Command Center (BDCC)

- Identify and equip a physical location to serve as the BDCC with necessary communication and coordination tools.
- Ensure the BDCC is operational round-the-clock during an active disaster response phase.

#### 2.2 Command and Control Structures

- Implement an incident command system to coordinate response activities, manage resources, and make critical decisions.
- Designate key personnel for roles such as Incident Commander, Operations Officer, and Logistics Officer.

#### 2.3 Communication Protocols

- Define standard operating procedures for internal and external communications during disasters.
- Employ multiple communication channels to ensure redundancy, such as radios, social media, and public address systems.

### Section 3: Emergency Response Operations

#### 3.1 Search and Rescue Operations

- Deploy trained search and rescue teams to locate and assist affected individuals.
- Prioritize areas with the highest risk and vulnerability based on the pre-disaster assessments.

#### 3.2 Medical Response and Triage Procedures

- Set up triage stations to provide immediate medical care to the injured.
- Coordinate with local hospitals and clinics for support and transfer of serious cases.

#### 3.3 Provision of Food, Water, and Shelter

- Distribute emergency supplies of food and water to affected individuals.
- Establish temporary shelters for displaced residents, ensuring they are safe and equipped with basic necessities.

### Section 4: Post-Disaster Assessment

#### 4.1 Damage Assessment Procedures

- Conduct rapid assessments of infrastructure damage and other impacts to determine immediate needs and resource allocation.
- Document findings for reference in recovery and rehabilitation efforts.

#### 4.2 Needs Assessment for Affected Populations

- Evaluate the needs of the affected community, including shelter, medical aid, and psychosocial support.
- Employ participatory approaches to accurately gauge the community's recovery requirements.

#### 4.3 Coordination with National Agencies

- Partner with national and international agencies for additional support and specialized services.
- Establish clear communication lines for requesting and managing extra assistance.

### Section 5: Recovery and Rehabilitation

#### 5.1 Short-Term and Long-Term Recovery Strategies

- Devise immediate response plans to address urgent community needs and initiate long-term recovery processes.
- Consider infrastructural repairs, economic recovery programs, and resumption of educational activities.

#### 5.2 Infrastructure Repair and Rebuilding Plans

- Prioritize and plan for the repair of critical infrastructure, such as bridges, roads, and public buildings.
- Apply "build back better" principles to increase the resilience of the reconstructed infrastructure.

#### 5.3 Psychosocial Support and Community Rebuilding Activities

- Offer psychosocial support to disaster survivors to foster mental well-being and social cohesion.
- Engage community members in rehabilitation projects to rebuild a sense of ownership and normalcy.

### Section 6: Conclusion

Reiterate the commitment to not only respond to disasters but also to ensure the recovery and return to normalcy of Barangay Tinago. Emphasize the need for ongoing evaluation, adaptation, and improvement of the emergency response and recovery protocols to stay current with best practices and the evolving risk landscape.
