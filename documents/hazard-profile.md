# Barangay Tinago Hazard Profile

## Introduction

This document provides a detailed hazard profile for Barangay Tinago, a vibrant community located in Ligao City. The purpose is to compile and assess geographical, historical, and socio-economic factors relevant to the barangay's disaster risk management efforts. The profile aims to serve as a foundational tool for informed decision-making in enhancing local resilience to potential hazards.

## Geographical Setting

Barangay Tinago is situated in the north-eastern region of Ligao City, spanning a total area of 5.2 square kilometers. The barangay has a varied topography, featuring flatlands suitable for agriculture, as well as hilly regions that provide natural scenic views. Given its location, Tinago experiences a tropical climate with distinct wet and dry seasons and is located at a moderate distance from the Mayon Volcano and several fault lines.

## Historical Hazard Incidents

Over the past decades, Barangay Tinago has faced numerous natural and man-made disasters. These include:

- Typhoons and severe tropical storms, notably Typhoon Reming, which caused widespread flooding and damage.
- Occasional earthquakes with varying magnitudes, due to the proximity to active fault lines.
- Minor landslides during intense rainfall periods, especially in the hilly areas.

A comprehensive analysis reveals an increased frequency of severe weather events, highlighting climate change implications. Past events have underscored the need for community-based disaster response and resilient infrastructure.

## Potential Hazards Identification

Barangay Tinago has identified several potential hazards that pose risks to the community:

- Natural Hazards:
  - Seasonal typhoons and related hazards such as heavy rainfall, flooding, and storm surges.
  - Earthquake risks from nearby fault lines.
  - Landslides in the hilly terrains during heavy rains.
- Man-made Hazards:
  - Fire hazards in densely populated residential and market areas.
  - Potential industrial accidents from nearby factories.
  - Transportation-related accidents on major thoroughfares.
- Health-Related Hazards:
  - Disease outbreaks and potential epidemics.
- Climate Change Risks:
  - Increased frequency and intensity of extreme weather events.
  - Slow-onset events such as sea-level rise and temperature changes.

## Vulnerability Assessment

Our assessment highlights vulnerable aspects within Barangay Tinago:

- High population density in low-lying areas vulnerable to flooding.
- Aging infrastructure not designed for extreme weather events.
- Socio-economically disadvantaged groups that may be less resilient to disasters.
- Limited access to healthcare facilities in remote areas.

## Mitigation Measures

Current mitigation measures in Barangay Tinago include:

- Established evacuation routes and emergency shelters.
- Regular disaster awareness and preparedness seminars.
- Zoning regulations designed to avoid settlement in high-risk areas.

However, several gaps have been identified:

- Lack of early warning systems and effective communication channels.
- Insufficient disaster-preparedness training for residents.
- Inadequate drainage systems that exacerbate flooding issues.

Recommendations for mitigating these hazards include:

- Upgrading of critical infrastructure to withstand severe weather conditions.
- Implementation of comprehensive land-use planning and strict enforcement of building codes.
- Expansion of community-based training programs focused on disaster response and preparedness.

In conclusion, this hazard profile for Barangay Tinago underscores the importance of proactive measures and community engagement in building a disaster-resilient community. Identifying potential hazards, assessing vulnerabilities, and implementing strategic mitigation initiatives will significantly aid in safeguarding the lives and livelihoods of residents against the inevitable challenges posed by natural and man-made disasters.
