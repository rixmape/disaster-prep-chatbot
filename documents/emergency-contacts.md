# Barangay Tinago Disaster Emergency Contacts

## Health Center

- Contact Number: 0956-745-2289 / 0917-123-4567
- Contact Person: Dr. Maria Dela Cruz, Chief Medical Officer
- Office Location: #123 Rizal Street, Barangay Tinago, Ligao City
- Operating Hours: Monday to Saturday, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM; for emergencies after hours, contact the Barangay Hall Hotline.
- Required Information: Symptoms being experienced, patient's age, any known allergies, and the current location within the barangay.
- Volunteer Opportunities: Trained individuals can volunteer for community health drives and first aid training sessions.

## Public Information and Warning System

- Contact Number: 0923-334-9845
- Contact Person: Ms. Liza Santos, Public Information Officer
- Office Location: Barangay Hall, #321 Bonifacio Street, Barangay Tinago, Ligao City
- Operating Hours: Information services from 9:00 AM to 8:00 PM, but warnings - are disseminated as needed anytime.
- Required Information: Non-emergency only. Inform about public announcements, disaster preparedness seminars, and feedback on information-dissemination.
- Volunteer Opportunities: Residents may volunteer to help disseminate information and education materials, especially during critical times.

## Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Office

- Contact Number: 0917-123-4567
- Contact Person: Mr. Juan Dela Cruz, DRRM Officer
- Office Location: Barangay Hall, #321 Bonifacio Street, Barangay Tinago, Ligao City
- Operating Hours: Office hours: 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM, coordination center operates 24/7.
- Required Information: Specific details of the emergency or threat, any observations of environmental changes, affected areas and potential risks.
- Volunteer Opportunities: Volunteers are welcomed for disaster risk awareness campaigns, evacuation drills, and emergency response team enrollment.

## Fire Brigade

- Contact Number: 0935-678-9101
- Contact Person: Fire Chief Alberto Reyes
- Office Location: Fire Station Rd., Barangay Tinago, Ligao City
- Operating Hours: 24/7 for emergency responses; office hours for non-emergency consultations from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM.
- Required Information: Exact location of fire or smoke, type of structure affected (residential, commercial, etc.), and if there are people trapped or in immediate danger.
- Volunteer Opportunities: Residents can volunteer for fire safety seminars, fire drills, and become part of the auxiliary fire response team.

## Police Station

- Contact Number: 0927-456-7890
- Contact Person: Police Chief Roberto Sanchez
- Office Location: Peacekeeper St., Barangay Tinago, Ligao City
- Operating Hours: 24/7 for emergency assistance; office hours for non-emergency reports from 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM.
- Required Information: Nature of the criminal activity, location, description of any suspects or vehicles involved, and if the situation is currently in progress.
- Volunteer Opportunities: Community members are encouraged to join neighborhood watch programs and participate in police-community partnership meetings.

## Utility Emergency Services

- Contact Number: 0945-213-4567 (for water), 0926-321-7654 (for power)
- Contact Person: Engr. Sofia Lopez (for water), Engr. Ricardo Torres (for power)
- Office Location: Utility Services Bldg., Barangay Tinago, Ligao City
- Operating Hours: 24/7 for restoration services following outages; customer service from 8:00 AM to 7:00 PM.

- Volunteer Opportunities: Community members can undergo training for emergency utility repairs and join quick-response teams.

## Local Search and Rescue (SAR) Unit

- Contact Number: 0918-654-3210
- Contact Person: SAR Coordinator Andrea Garcia
- Office Location: Adjacent to the Barangay Hall, Barangay Tinago, Ligao City
- Operating Hours: 24/7 availability for emergency search and rescue operations.
- Required Information: Information on the missing person(s) or distress situation, last known location, time last seen, and weather conditions in the area.
- Volunteer Opportunities: Residents can train to be part of the SAR team or support staff during operations.

## Environmental Monitoring Team

- Contact Number: 0975-987-6543
- Contact Person: Environmental Officer Mark Bautista
- Office Location: Environmental Offices, Barangay Tinago, Ligao City
- Operating Hours: Monitoring conducted 24/7; office hours for reports and inquiries from 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM.

- Volunteer Opportunities: Volunteers can assist in regular environmental patrols and participate in clean-up drives or ecological projects.

## Emergency Transportation Service

- Contact Number: 0967-123-9876
- Contact Person: Transportation Head Carlos Dimaguila
- Office Location: Public Transport Hub, Barangay Tinago, Ligao City
